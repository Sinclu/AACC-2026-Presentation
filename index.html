Executive Summary Snapshot

Key Insight

While enthusiasm for AI is high (71%), many institutions are still stuck in the harder middle stage of adoption. The largest group of respondents placed their institutions in the Trough of Disillusionment. The results suggest that progress depends less on the technology itself and more on closing a capacity gap by giving faculty the time, training, and support they need to move past fear and uncertainty.
